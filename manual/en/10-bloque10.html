LABG Suite: where the work continues

SigmaPro is part of the LABG Suite, a set of free apps that run in the browser. Some work begins or ends outside SigmaPro: planning and randomizing the design, exploring many variables at once, reducing dimensions, forming groups; and the suite reaches beyond statistics, from phenology and forest inventory to population genetics and phylogeny. This chapter explains the LABG Suite block, the suggestions that appear within your work and how to take the data from one app to another.

10.1The LABG Suite block

The block presents all of SigmaPro’s sister apps, 22 of them, grouped by area as on the suite portal (table 10.1). At the end of the Home block, the same box, “Explore the LABG suite”, shows only the four that most often continue an agronomic analysis: PCAPro, ClusteringPro, AgriDesign and CorrelationPro.

Each card has:

  • The app’s official logo, which is also a link to it, its name and its published version (or Online, when the app does not publish a version number).
  • Its tagline and four to six features, taken from each app’s published documentation.
  • Open app and Manual, which open the app or its manual in a new browser tab; an app without an online manual has only the first.
  • Cite, which unfolds the citation in APA format and in BibTeX, with buttons to copy it (section 10.6).

iNote

The suite’s apps open in your browser, with nothing to install, and process the data on your computer, like SigmaPro. Opening one of them does not send it anything: SigmaPro does not send your data anywhere.

10.2Suggestions within your work

Outside block 10, the suite’s apps appear only where they continue what you are doing, in a discreet strip titled Continue in the LABG suite. Never in pop-up windows and never as advertising.

Each card carries the app’s logo as a link (it opens in a new tab), a sentence that says why the app is useful there, Send data to … when the app is already published (10.4) and a ✕ button to hide the card (10.5). The strips appear in three places:

  • At the foot of the result of an analysis, in the Analysis block, and in section 9 of its Fundamentals fact sheet.
  • At the foot of a family in the Fundamentals catalog, as Related tools.
  • In the Data block, when the loaded table has a structure that another app can make use of: a design with treatments and blocks, many numeric variables, coordinates or dates. The card says what it saw in the table (“The table has 8 numeric variables”).

10.4Sending the data to another app

Send data to … sends nothing over the internet: it saves the Data block’s table as a CSV file on your computer and opens the other app in a new tab.

  1. With the table loaded, press Send data to PCAPro (or to the corresponding app).
  2. The browser downloads sigmapro-para-pcapro.csv and opens PCAPro; a notice reminds you of the file name.
  3. In the other app, open that file from its data block.

The file contains the table SigmaPro is using (with the active filter, if there is one): the numbers with all their digits, each factor with the names of its levels and missing values as empty cells, in UTF-8 so that accented characters arrive intact. For apps that work with numeric variables, such as PCAPro, the file has a label column (the first non-numeric one) and all the numeric ones. If no data are loaded, the button says so and downloads nothing.

10.5Hiding a suggestion

The ✕ button on a card hides it in that place (for example, PCAPro at the foot of the regression), and SigmaPro remembers this in this browser. The same app keeps appearing in the other places.

To bring them all back, the LABG Suite block has Show again the suggestions you hid below the cards. If all the cards of a strip are hidden, the whole strip disappears.

10.6How to cite the apps

If you used another app of the suite in your work, cite it the way you cite SigmaPro (Home block and appendix E): with its version and its DOI.

Cite unfolds the app’s citation in APA format and in BibTeX, built from the app’s published data (its citation file and its metadata), so it changes with each version; Copy APA and Copy BibTeX copy it to the clipboard. An app without a DOI is cited with its web address, and one that does not publish a version number, without it. For example, for PCAPro:

✎Example

Barrera-Guzmán, L. Á. (2026). PCAPro: an offline, browser-based platform for guided principal component analysis (Version 1.1.0) [Computer software]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22649709
