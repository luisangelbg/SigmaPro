Block 6 · Figures

Figures: ready to publish

The charts of the analyses in the report come together in this block, numbered and with their captions, in the order of the report. Here you choose the error bars, the chart theme and the width, download them as SVG, vector PDF, PNG or TIFF at 600 dpi, touch them up with the ✎ editor and, for the exact size a journal asks for, open them in the Figure Studio.

6.1The figures in the report

The block shows all the figures of the analyses in the report that have charts (designs, comparisons, regression, correlation, contingency and almost all the others), in a gallery of cards. If the report has none, the block says so and offers to take you to Results.

Each card says which analysis it comes from (“From analysis 2: Mean comparison: Tukey · rend”) and shows the figure with its caption: the number (the same one it has in the report and in the exported document), the title and a description of what it shows, which is also the figure’s alternative text for screen readers. In mean plots, the caption states the error bars (“with ± one standard error”), as journals require.

6.2Error bars, theme and width

Above the gallery, three settings apply to every figure in the report: on screen, in the exported document and in the downloads. The app remembers them in the browser.

  • Error bars: ± 1 SE (the standard error of each mean, the default) or 95% CI (the confidence interval). When you change them, the mean plots are redrawn and their captions change.
  • Chart theme: the look of the figures, among the six in table 6.1. The theme applies to every chart in the app: the same selector appears above the charts of each analysis in Analysis and in Results (also in Check the model assumptions), and changing any one of them changes them all.
  • Width in the document and in the downloads: 8.5 cm (one column), 12 cm, 16 cm (page width, the default) or 17.5 cm (two columns). The height follows from each figure’s proportions.
Table 6.1. The chart themes.
ThemeHow the figures look
SigmaPro (gold and black)The default: text and lines in black (in white with the app in dark mode), gold accents and a faint grid.
Minimal (grays, no grid)Grays only, no grid and thin axes.
Grid (gray panel)The plot area in gray with the grid in white and no axis lines; accents in blue.
Journal (black and white, serif)Black and white, a serif typeface (Times New Roman), no grid and black axes: the style many print journals ask for.
Color-blind safe (Okabe-Ito palette)The series in the Okabe and Ito palette (blue, orange, green, pink and sky blue), whose colors can be told apart with any type of color blindness.
Presentation (large text)Larger text and thicker lines, for projecting in a room.

The theme changes the colors, the text and the lines, not the numbers: each figure shows the same data with any of the six. Whatever you touched up with the ✎ editor (section 6.4) is kept when you change the theme.

6.3The downloads

Each card downloads its figure at the chosen width in four formats. All of them come out with the chosen chart theme, in its light version even if the app is in dark mode, and with whatever you changed with the ✎ editor or the studio.

FormatWhat it is and what it is for
SVGVector, with the size in millimeters. It opens and can be edited in any vector drawing program, and it scales up without losing sharpness.
PDFVector, with lines and text in one of the standard PDF fonts (Helvetica; Times with the Journal theme): it is small, it scales up without losing sharpness and its text is searchable. It is the format many journals prefer.
PNG 600 dpiAn image at 600 dots per inch, with the resolution written inside the file: it goes into a word processor at its correct size.
TIFF 600 dpiThe same image in TIFF, the format some journals and publishers require.

To download all the figures at once, in a ZIP with the four formats, use the Export block (chapter 7).

6.4The ✎ editor

Every SigmaPro figure, here, in Analysis and in Results, has a ✎ button in its corner that opens a floating panel to touch up that figure, as in a charting program.

TabWhat you change
GeneralA title, a subtitle and a footnote inside the figure; the font family, the size and the color of all the text; the line weight and the point size; the background and a frame.
Axes & gridThe axis titles, the tick labels, the color and weight of the axes, the grid and the box around the plot area.
SeriesEach color the figure uses, with the name of its legend entry: color, weight, stroke, opacity, or hiding it.
LegendShow or hide it, eight positions, as it is, in a row or in a column, its size and its box; you can also drag it on the figure.
TextsEach text in the figure, one by one: its wording, size, bold, italics, color, or hiding it.
AnnotationsNotes, arrows, reference lines at a value of any of the axes, shaded bands and the panel letter (A, B…); notes and arrows can be dragged.

The changes are written into the figure itself, so what you see is what gets exported. The browser remembers them for each figure and reapplies them every time the figure is redrawn (when the data, the error bars or the theme change). Undo everything on this figure returns it to how SigmaPro drew it, and Copy the style to every figure carries the general settings to the others: the font, the sizes, the text color, the background, the frame and the style of the axes and the grid.

6.5The Figure Studio

To give a figure the exact size a journal asks for, Open in the studio takes it to the LABG Figure Studio, the same one in every app of the suite: a split screen with the figure on the left, to scale and with the outline of its output size, and the inspector on the right.

  • Journal sizes: one, one and a half or two columns, in millimeters or inches, with text size and line weight designed for the output size. Below the figure, the final size and the number of pixels it will have.
  • Scientific palettes, with a simulation of how a person with color blindness would see the figure.
  • The legend inside the chart (eight places), outside on the right, below in a row or hidden, and you can drag it on the figure.
  • SigmaPro’s settings, inside: the error bars, the width and the chart theme from the Figures block appear in the inspector, under Figure settings (each change is made in the app), and the ✎ editor is docked as “Fine editing of this figure”.
  • Comfortable work: undo and redo, before-and-after comparison with a curtain (B key), zoom, double-click on a text to change it, a control search, one-click presets (and your own, saved as JSON) and the ? key to see the shortcuts.
  • Export: PNG, SVG, PDF and TIFF, at 300, 600 or 1200 dpi, with a transparent background if you want one. For the PDF, the studio asks SigmaPro for the vector figure instead of an image.

The studio remembers its settings in the browser and changes nothing in the app: when you close it, the figure goes back to its place with whatever was done to it. If for some reason it cannot open, the app keeps working as usual, with the ✎ editor and the block’s downloads.
